On Earth, a flame stretches up because hot air rises. In orbit nothing rises, and a flame lives or dies on the air moving past it. MicroFire Atlas turns NASA's real microgravity fire experiments into an adventure for explorers aged 8 to 13, with the full evidence underneath for mentors and judges.

What's inside
- 78 NASA test records: 56 BASS-II tests, 20 Saffire spacecraft-fire runs and 2 LUCI burns in simulated lunar gravity, each value traced to its NASA page
- 62 NASA findings, each quote checked word for word at build time
- Explorer mode: quests and stars, a data-detective table, predict-first comparisons and the Evidence Ladder game
- Mission Analyst mode: NASA's exploration atmospheres, a coverage instrument over the real evidence, and an evidence status that never guesses or scores risk
- Rankings re-run 1,000 ways; the AI graded on 100 questions (98 pass before the model; 99.9 % citation precision in a live run)
- FM², NASA's planned burn on the Moon, shown as a research horizon with no invented results

Characters, scenery and the opening explainer are illustrations. NASA footage, photographs and test records are evidence. Not affiliated with or endorsed by NASA.
